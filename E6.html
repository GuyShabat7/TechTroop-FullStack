Classic Shakshuka

  • 4 fresh eggs
  • 1 can of crushed tomatoes
  • 1 red bell pepper, chopped
  • 2 cloves of garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • Olive oil, salt, and pepper

the process:

  1. Sauté the bell pepper, and garlic in olive oil until soft.
  2. Pour in the crushed tomatoes and spices. Let it simmer for 10 minutes.
  3. Make small wells in the sauce and crack the eggs into them.
  4. Cover the pan and cook until the eggs are set. Enjoy!
